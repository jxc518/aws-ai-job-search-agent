How It Works

From job discovery to governed AI review

The platform separates collection, eligibility, machine-learning ranking and generative-AI review so each layer has a clear responsibility and validation boundary.

1

Discover

Employer-specific connectors collect official career listings and normalize them into a common job structure.

2

Govern

Profile-aware rules evaluate job category, user eligibility and government-contractor clearance requirements.

3

Rank

Amazon SageMaker produces an ML ranking signal to help prioritize opportunities for review.

4

Review

Amazon Bedrock generates an evidence-grounded review while preserving limitations and human-review controls.

Engineering Capabilities

Built for extensibility, traceability and governance

MULTI-PROFILE

Profile isolation

Separate role families, search policies and eligibility constraints for different users.

CONNECTORS

Company-specific adapters

Employer-specific collection logic sits behind a normalized downstream job contract.

MACHINE LEARNING

ML-based ranking

SageMaker adds a model-based prioritization signal without presenting it as a calibrated hiring probability.

GENERATIVE AI

Evidence-grounded review

Bedrock reviews verified project evidence and carries explicit evidence limitations forward.

GOVERNANCE

Clearance-aware policy

Public Trust and approved low-level requirements can be distinguished from higher-clearance roles.

VALIDATION

E2E contract validation

Identity, source truth, model limitations, governance status and provenance are validated across layers.

Technical Architecture

Architecture-ready without over-coupling the platform

Profile Config→ Company Connectors→ Canonical Job Data→ AWS Lambda→ Amazon S3→ SageMaker+ Bedrock→ Governed Result

New employers can be added through connector boundaries; profile and job-family rules remain centrally managed. Proven source, ML, GenAI and governance evidence is carried forward rather than silently reinterpreted downstream.
